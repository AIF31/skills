## Subagents

Read the T3 adapter's Subagent routing and Model policy before spawning. Read `{{MODELS}}` once per session and again after a configuration edit. Resolve the configured role, documentation override, or independent-review direction before choosing a tool. Every brief names the goal, artifact and author model, evidence, permitted writes, and completion check.

The root runs the playbook. Helpers read `{{PSTACK}}/agents/poteto-agent.md` by path; routed skills can name their own prompt files instead. Native helpers must satisfy T3's provider, settings, checkout, and lifetime restrictions. User-chosen settings go through `delegate_task`, with options in `target.options`.

Use the configured role's starting effort and adapt it to the job under the saved ceiling. Explicit medium and high role settings override the general budget preset. Preserve model identity, panel count, and review independence. Report unavailable choices rather than substituting another model. Standard speed is the default; an urgency override requires a supported option.

Independent reviews use a fresh agent from the family opposite the artifact's author: apply `review of GPT work` or `review of Claude work`, not the main chat's family. Ordinary reviews use one reviewer; named panel skills retain their configured seats. Documentation and explanatory writing use `documentation`, unless the user explicitly requests the current thread model or another model. Keep implementation, investigations, and judgment on their own roles.
